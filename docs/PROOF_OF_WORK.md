## The gap this work exposed, and what OSI does about it

The record below is real, paid, and validated by an open market. It is also a
case study in the exact problem OSI closes.

| Property of the existing model | What it costs | What OSI does instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Scattered.** Spread across dozens of separate transactions and private buyers. | No analyst can point to one place and say "this is my work". | One public, wallet-bound profile that accumulates every attributable contribution. |
| **Non-portable.** The track record belongs to the marketplace, not the analyst. | Leaving the platform means starting over. | Analyst standing is a Solana Attestation Service credential on mainnet. It is verifiable without OSI and survives OSI. |
| **Unreviewed in the open.** Reports are sold finished. The reasoning behind a conclusion is invisible and cannot be contested. | A buyer takes the conclusion on trust. A subject has no way to answer it. | Immutable versions, independent weighted review, published reviewer identities and decisions, and a standing right to challenge. |
| **Invisible to the people who need it most.** Paid, closed marketplaces do not serve drained retail victims. | The people with the least money get the least forensics. | Free public intake, free public records, non-custodial and optional rewards. |

OSI is the platform its maintainer wanted to exist for that work, built so the
next analyst does not have to prove themselves one private sale at a time.

and could not be deleted or replaced afterwards, so it appears as lost on the
listing. The finished work was a collaborative submission with a teammate, and
the credit for that contribution is public here:
[x.com/Cipher0091/status/1917653761519976605](https://x.com/Cipher0091/status/1917653761519976605).
It is recorded this way rather than quietly omitted, because a track record
that hides its messy entries is not a track record.

That failure is also, precisely, an OSI design input. A submission that cannot
be corrected, cannot be superseded by a new version, and carries no public
provenance is exactly the failure mode OSI's immutable-version model exists to
prevent: in OSI a mistake is never deleted and never overwritten, it is
superseded by a new signed version while the original stays visible.
